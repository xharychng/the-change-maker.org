Our mission

We give junior and senior high school students a floor to debate international issues with each other, and the room to grow into leaders who can speak, listen and act on a global scale.

The Change Makers started in 2025 as a team made up mostly of high school students. Before running a conference of our own, our members helped organize Model United Nations events with Kang Chiao International School, Taipei Canadian School, Taoyuan Municipal Taoyuan Senior High School and the Affiliated Senior High School of National Taiwan Normal University (HSNU).

CMMUN I is the first conference we are building from scratch, and we want it to be the one that gets more students paying attention to the world beyond their classroom.

What we do

Three goals shape every committee, session and role at CMMUN.

Debate and exchange

Students from different schools and language backgrounds share one floor, argue their positions and trade ideas, in both Chinese and English committees.

Leadership in practice

As delegates, chairs and staff, students practice what a classroom rarely tests: leading a room, communicating under pressure, working as a team and speaking in public.

A global perspective

We ask students to read international issues through the lens of information, media and technology, and then to stand up and propose solutions of their own.
