About the conference

CMMUN I (青年領航者模擬聯合國) is a Model United Nations conference organized entirely by students, for junior and senior high school students across Taiwan. Delegates debate current international affairs on the floor, negotiate in caucus, draft resolutions and vote on them.

Chinese and English committees run separately, so every delegate can argue in the language they think in. This year's theme centers on international issues tied to information and technology (provisional). Expect 80 to 100 delegates, in single and double delegations, led by two Secretaries-General, a four-person chair team in every committee and an executive team of about ten. Day one closes with a networking banquet for everyone.

Questions

Do I need MUN experience to join?

No. Our beginner committees are built for first-time delegates, and your chairs will walk you through procedure. Experienced delegates can choose an advanced committee instead.

How are committee assignments decided?

You rank your committee choices and list country preferences when you register. Our Academics team assigns committees and countries from those preferences and announces them before the conference.

Can I join with a partner?

Yes. You can register as a single delegate or as a double delegation with a partner. Put your partner's name in the registration form.

Will there be awards?

Yes. Each committee's chairs present awards at the closing ceremony on day two.
