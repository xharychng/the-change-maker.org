Join CMMUN

There are three ways to take part. Check who can apply and when each form is open, then fill in that position's form.

Chairs Delegates Staff
I
Applications open

Chairs

Join a committee's four-person chair team as a co-chair or assistant chair: moderate debate, help write the chair report and background guide, and judge delegates.

Deadline: December 7, 2026, 11:59 pm

Who can apply

  • Junior high school students and above
  • Experience as an MUN delegate; chairing experience helps but isn't required
  • We need chairs for both the Chinese and the English committees

What to prepare

  • Your certificate name, exactly as it appears on your passport
  • A list of your MUN conferences and the role you held at each
  • Two short essays: a challenge you'd face as a chair (100–150 words) and how far diplomacy has succeeded this century (200–250 words)
  • Shortlisted applicants receive an email with interview details by December 13
II
Applications open

Delegates

Represent a country in committee: debate the topic, negotiate with other delegations and work toward a resolution that passes the vote.

Registration: January 1 – March 1, 2027, 11:59 pm

Who can apply

  • Junior and senior high school students from any school in Taiwan
  • No MUN experience needed; beginner committees welcome first-timers
  • Register as a single delegate or as a double delegation with a partner

What to prepare

  • Your committee and country preferences
  • The delegate fee: payment instructions go live when registration opens
  • A position paper, due before the conference
III
Applications open

Staff

Keep the conference running from the inside, as a photographer who captures both days or as a pager who carries notes between delegates during sessions. Every staff member earns volunteer service hours.

Registration: January 1 – March 1, 2027, 11:59 pm

Who can apply

  • Junior and senior high school students from any school in Taiwan
  • No MUN experience needed
  • Photographers: your own camera helps, but isn't required

What to prepare

  • Your certificate name, exactly as it appears on your passport
  • Which role you want: photographer or pager
  • The conference days you can attend
  • Volunteer service hours are issued after the conference
